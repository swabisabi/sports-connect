# Pitch and poster (Pitch lead)

## Poster
`python tools/make_poster.py ops/poster.pdf`. Don't print until the final address and the till number are set. Scan the QR on a phone first.

## Pitch line
Finding a team is free. Getting to your first game within a week costs KSh 200, or you get it back.

## Five slides, 3 minutes
1. **Problem (30s):** you move, you know nobody, teams exist but you can't see them.
2. **Live demo (75s):** scan, intro form, join group, pinned listing, pass booking, connector confirmation.
3. **Real numbers (25s):** joined ___, intros ___, offered pass ___, paid ___, attended ___.
4. **Money (35s):** the pass; founding sponsor (proposed KSh 7,500 a month); featured listings as the next stage. Costs KSh 6,500 a month (assumption).
5. **Next (15s):** first sponsor, more admins, second town.

## An example month (assumptions, not forecasts)
30 of 100 newcomers buy a pass: 6,000. Less 3 refunds and 10 referral rewards: -1,100. One sponsor: 7,500. 5 featured listings at 1,000: 5,000. Total 17,400; less costs 6,500 = 10,900, before payment charges and pay for team time.
